Good to know

Frequently asked

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings we recommend three to six months so venues and suppliers are still open on your date. Showers, birthdays and graduations are usually comfortable with four to six weeks. Funeral setups are arranged at short notice — message us on WhatsApp and we will confirm availability the same day.

Do you travel between Johannesburg and Limpopo?

Yes. We are based in Johannesburg and Limpopo and work across both. Travel outside those areas is quoted upfront and confirmed with you before anything is booked.

Can I book just one service?

Absolutely. Many clients book only décor, only flowers, or only hair and makeup. Select what you need on the form and we will quote just that.

How does payment work?

A deposit secures your date and the suppliers we book on your behalf, with the balance settled before the event. The exact split is set out on your quote so there are no surprises.

Are the prices on the services page final?

They are our partners’ current list prices and are accurate for standard bookings. Final costs depend on your guest count, venue, travel and any custom detail — all itemised on your quote.
